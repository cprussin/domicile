The clipboard history is wider and shows more of each copy: up to four lines of text with its line breaks, and a larger image preview beside its size. What is on your clipboard gets its own group above what was copied before. The compositor previews 480 characters and makes thumbnails up to 256 px. `RowTile` drops its `picture` prop.
