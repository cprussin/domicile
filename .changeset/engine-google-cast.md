The engine casts the desktop to Google Cast TVs: `domicile.googleCast` lists them, and casts to them and stops. `engineCast(domicile.googleCast)` in `@domicile-desktop/sdk/cast` is its backend, and the `Cast` bar item uses it, so it shows when a TV is near.
