The engine casts the desktop to Google Cast TVs: `domicile.castSinks` lists them, and `startCasting` and `stopCasting` run casts. `engineCast` in `@domicile-desktop/sdk/cast` is its backend, and the `Cast` bar item uses it, so it shows when a TV is near.
