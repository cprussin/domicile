A browser window's zoom popup no longer closes when Reset is pressed, and stays up while the pointer is anywhere over it. Its zoom and downloads popups close when another window takes the focus or another tab covers the page. A download's name runs into the room its buttons take until the pointer comes. `Popover` takes `onHoverChange`.
