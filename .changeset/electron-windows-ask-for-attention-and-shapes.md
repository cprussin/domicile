An Electron app's window can ask for the user's attention, let the pointer through and take a shape (`flash_frame`, `set_ignore_mouse_events`, `set_shape`), listed to the shell with the window; the mouse's back and forward buttons reach the app (`app_command`); a page captures one of its app's windows by its `getMediaSourceId`; and an app opens `about:blank` and `data:` pages.
