The top bar's sharing indicator says what is shared. It is a red "Sharing screen" pill, and its panel lists each source by name with what of it is shared: a window with its application, a screen by make and model, all screens, or part of a screen with its size. A region covering one whole screen is listed as that screen, and one covering every screen as all of them, instead of "Region". `Captured` carries a window's app id, a monitor's description, the monitor a region is on, and a new `Desk` kind.
