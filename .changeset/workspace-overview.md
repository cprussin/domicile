Manganese has an overview: each monitor's workspaces at once, with their live windows. Open it with Meta+O, a four-finger pinch in, or the new button before the workspaces in the bar. Click a workspace or a window to go there, or drag a window onto another workspace to move it. A four-finger pinch out opens the launcher, whose button now shows a sparkle that twinkles when pointed at. The component library adds `AnimatedSparkleIcon`.
