Bar items ease in, out and between widths: each sits in a new `FluidItem`, which follows its content's width, fades as it comes and goes, and keeps what left drawn until it has faded. Quick settings' icons do the same. `QuickSettingsParts.Indicators` is now a list of components, one per icon.
