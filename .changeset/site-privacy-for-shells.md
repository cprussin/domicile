A `<webview>` reports how its page blocks ads and trackers and how many of its requests were blocked (`privacySite`, `blockAdsAndTrackers`, `blockAdsAndTrackersSetFor`, `blockedCount`, `domicile-site-privacy-change`), and `setBlockAdsAndTrackers(mode, "site" | "window")` changes it for the page's site, kept in its profile, or for its window until it closes.
