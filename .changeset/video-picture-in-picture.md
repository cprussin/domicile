Picture-in-picture video works: a video's picture-in-picture button, or a site's, floats the video over your windows in the corner of its screen. Drag it to move it, drag an edge to resize it, and point at it for play, pause, track, back-to-tab and close controls. Shells list the videos in `domicile.pictureInPicture` and show one with `<app picture-in-picture="id">`.
