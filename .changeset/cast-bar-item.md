A `Cast` bar item lists the TVs nearby: extend the desktop onto one, or cast what the share picker picks to it. `@domicile-desktop/sdk/cast` runs the casts, and `<PortalDialogs />` answers a cast's own ScreenCast request. No backend finds TVs yet, so the item stays hidden.
