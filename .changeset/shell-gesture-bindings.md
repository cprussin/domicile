Shells bind touchpad gestures of three fingers or more beside their keys, in sway's `bindgesture` grammar such as `swipe:3:left`, and in the same modes. `DomicileHost.grabGesture()` and the `gesture` event carry every phase; `domicile.gestures` wraps them. Manganese swipes three fingers between a monitor's workspaces, and gains `workspace next_on_output` and `prev_on_output`.
