Window and browser icons move too. Tabs and title bars: Close snips, New tab reaches out, Float's windows lift apart and gather into Tile's squares, Maximize's corners push out and travel into Restore's, and a group's new layout builds in. Browser toolbar: Back and Forward dart, Reload winds and spins into Stop, zoom's minus and plus draw in and reach out, the lock snaps shut on a secure page, site settings' knobs slide, and each new download drops into its tray. New `AnimatedCloseIcon`, `AnimatedPlusIcon`, `AnimatedMinusIcon`, `AnimatedFloatIcon`, `AnimatedMaximizeIcon`, `AnimatedLayoutIcon`, `AnimatedCaretIcon`, `AnimatedReloadIcon`, `AnimatedLockIcon`, `AnimatedSlidersIcon` and `AnimatedDownloadIcon`, and a `direction` for icon parts.
