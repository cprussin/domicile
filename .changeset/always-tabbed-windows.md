Every tiled window is in a tab group, so a new window always opens as a tab. Splitting is a move: Meta+B / Meta+V move the window out of its group to the right / below, Meta+E splits a group's tabs into groups side by side, and Meta+W on a selected split merges its groups. Meta+Shift+arrow moves a window alone in its group into the next group as a tab.
