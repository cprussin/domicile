Move windows on springs: opening, closing, tab contents, restacking, the scratchpad, workspace switches and picture-in-picture. A workspace switch turned back midway reverses from where it is, a tab switch slides the revealed tab in from its side, and a moving tab's ring and shadow fade as a layer of their own.
