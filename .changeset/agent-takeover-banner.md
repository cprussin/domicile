Taking a window from an agent says so plainly. The window's ring turns amber and its tag reads "Paused", a banner slides down under its title bar with Give back and Hide, the agent's pointer turns gray there, and the agent pill turns amber and drops a notice for a few seconds. Rings fade in and out. Adds the `agentPaused` color token.
