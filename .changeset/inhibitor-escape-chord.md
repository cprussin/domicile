A shell can give you a way back from a VM or remote desktop viewer that holds your shortcuts. A chord bound `--inhibited Meta+Escape` stays the shell's while that window has the keyboard, and `keyboard.switchShortcutsInhibitor` turns the window's hold off and on. Manganese has a `shortcuts-inhibitor toggle` command to bind to it.
