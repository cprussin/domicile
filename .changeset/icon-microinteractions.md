Icons in the bar, quick settings and level notices move when their state changes or they are pointed at: the bell swings, Wi-Fi arcs fill out, Bluetooth dots slide out, speaker waves pulse, a slash draws across what is off, the dark mode sun turns to a moon, and more. New `Animated*Icon` components and `_controlHover` / `_controlActive` conditions in the component library. The notifications bar item no longer exports a Panda preset.
