Apps can describe their colors with `wp_color_management_v1`: wide-gamut (Display P3, Adobe RGB, BT.2020) and HDR (PQ, HLG, scRGB) windows are drawn in the colors they mean rather than as sRGB. Every display still describes itself as sRGB until HDR output lands.
