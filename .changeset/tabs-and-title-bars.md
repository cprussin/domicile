Tabs, title bars and the browser's address bar share one look. Titles are 13px with 16px icons. The open tab, the focused title bar and the browser toolbar are one surface (white in light mode) on a darker tab strip, which resting title bars match. A tab shows its float and maximize buttons only under the pointer, and a hidden tab its close button, so titles get the room. The address field is a 13px, tinted, edgeless field until clicked; the padlock is gray; zoom controls show only while a page is zoomed. `Autocomplete` takes `filled`.
