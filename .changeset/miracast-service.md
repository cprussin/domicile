Cast the desktop to Miracast TVs (Fire TV, Roku, Samsung, LG, Windows wireless displays) over Wi-Fi Direct or the LAN. The new `domicile-miracast` service runs each cast; `miracastCast(domicile)` in `@domicile-desktop/sdk/miracast` is its backend, and the `Cast` bar item lists its TVs.
