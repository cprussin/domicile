Shells bind touchscreen swipes in from a screen's edge, written `edge:left`, `edge:right`, `edge:top`, `edge:bottom` or `edge`, beside their keys and touchpad gestures. A finger landing at an edge the shell binds waits until it is clearly coming in; a scroll or tap there still reaches the app underneath. Manganese swipes between a monitor's workspaces from its side edges.
