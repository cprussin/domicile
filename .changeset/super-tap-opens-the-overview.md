Tapping Super on its own opens and closes the overview, as it does on GNOME and KDE. Holding Super for a shortcut, a drag or a scroll still works as before. Shells can bind a tap of any modifier alone by writing just its name, such as `Meta` or `Alt`, in their keybindings.
