Manganese's touchpad gestures: a three-finger swipe moves focus that way, as Meta+H/J/K/L do. Four fingers now switch workspaces (left and right), and pull the scratchpad down or push it back up (down and up). Shells can bind the scratchpad's two directions with the new `scratchpad bring` and `scratchpad hide` commands.
